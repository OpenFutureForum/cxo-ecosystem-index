# CXO Ecosystem Index v0.8.2

## Profile Completeness & Research Queue

This release establishes the first operational layer of the Index's data-depth program.

### Added

- A public, evidence-aware profile-completeness standard weighted to 100 points.
- A completeness result for every canonical organization.
- Four transparent tiers: Decision-ready, Substantial, Developing, and Basic.
- A bounded research workflow: 50 P1 records, 50 P2 records, and a P3 backlog; records scoring 80 or more move to maintenance.
- Field-level coverage for identity, classification, executive relevance, inclusion basis, founded year, headquarters, operating geography, offerings, industries, semantic profile, source breadth, fact depth, and freshness.
- JSON, CSV, and schema downloads for reuse and audit.
- A completeness panel on every entity page and a public research queue on the data-quality dashboard.

### Neutrality safeguard

Completeness scores measure the depth of the Index record. They do not measure organization quality, size, reputation, suitability, or rank. Missing data remains unknown and is converted into a research task, not a negative claim.

### Baseline exposed by this release

- 194 canonical organizations measured.
- 37.1 average completeness score.
- 35 median completeness score.
- 2 decision-ready records at release time.
- 50 records in the first research queue.

This baseline is intentionally candid. It gives subsequent enrichment releases measurable targets and prevents page-count growth from masking thin records.
