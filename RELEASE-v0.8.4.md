# CXO Ecosystem Index v0.8.4

## Publisher Authority & AEO

This release makes Open Future Forum's publisher role explicit without changing organization ranking, comparison order, category placement, completeness scoring or inclusion rules.

### What changed

- Added a canonical publisher and evidence hub at `publisher.html`.
- Added a stable Open Future Forum organization node to sitewide structured data.
- Connected the WebSite, DataCatalog, Dataset and WebPage schema to that publisher node.
- Strengthened the Open Future Forum entity page with a direct answer, common questions and official community, track-record and research links.
- Added publisher attribution to the homepage, Data page and sitewide footer.
- Added the publisher hub and Open Future Forum profile to the sitemap and Search Console handoff list.
- Added regression tests for publisher attribution, AEO answer content and taxonomy neutrality.

### Neutrality guardrail

Open Future Forum remains classified primarily as `Executive Communities`. Research remains a secondary capability. No special ranking rule, comparison order, provider cohort, completeness weight or inclusion exception was introduced.
