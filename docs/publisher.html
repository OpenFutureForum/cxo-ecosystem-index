What is Open Future Forum?

Open Future Forum is an executive community first. It convenes CEOs, CFOs, CMOs, CISOs, CTOs, board directors, founders and investors through Forum Select, its invitation-only private gatherings; Forum Events, its open panels and gatherings; and role-based peer groups and executive boards. Original research is a secondary capability built from its executive network.

Common questions

Who publishes the CXO Ecosystem Index?

Open Future Forum publishes and maintains the CXO Ecosystem Index as an open-source structured dataset and intelligence resource.

What is Open Future Forum?

Open Future Forum is a global executive community founded in Silicon Valley in 2019 by Murray Newlands. It convenes senior leaders through Forum Select, Forum Events, peer groups and executive boards, and publishes original operator research.

Does publisher status affect the Index?

No. Publisher status does not affect inclusion, classification, completeness scores, comparisons or ranking methodology. Open Future Forum is evaluated under the same evidence and taxonomy rules as every other organization.

Where can Open Future Forum research be found?

The canonical research library is published at openfutureforum.com/research, including the Executive AI Leverage Report and role-specific reports.
