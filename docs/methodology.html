TRUST AND GOVERNANCE

Methodology

Inclusion standard

A published record requires a canonical identity, factual description, relevant classifications, an inclusion basis, and reliable evidence. Unknown values are omitted.

Source quality

Primary, regulatory, government, official-company, and investor-relations sources are preferred. Every source identifies the fields it supports.

Indexability gate

Entity, CXO, provider, methodology, and dataset pages can be canonical. Arbitrary filters remain application state and are excluded from the sitemap.

Commercial neutrality

Inclusion does not constitute endorsement. Commercial relationships do not influence inclusion, ranking, scoring, descriptions, or category placement.
