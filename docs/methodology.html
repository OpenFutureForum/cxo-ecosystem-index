TRUST AND GOVERNANCE

Methodology

Classification model

Organizations are classified independently across executive roles, provider types, community formats, event formats, intelligence types, resources, executive needs, topics, and geographies. A multi-category organization retains one canonical entity record.

Controlled vocabulary

Every semantic term has a stable ID and neutral definition. Closely related phrases map to one canonical concept unless they represent a material distinction.

Evidence requirement

Entity associations require reasonable evidence from official organization, event, research, regulatory, government, or reputable third-party sources. A passing keyword mention is insufficient.

Indexability gate

Arbitrary filter combinations remain application state. A canonical page is created only when it has stable intent, enough substantive entities, distinct analysis, and durable value.

Commercial neutrality

Inclusion does not constitute endorsement. Commercial relationships do not influence inclusion, ranking, scoring, descriptions, or category placement.

Versioning

Schema and dataset versions are published in exports. Stable IDs do not change without a documented migration.
