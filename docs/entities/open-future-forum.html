Common questions

What is Open Future Forum?

Open Future Forum is a global executive community founded in Silicon Valley in 2019. It convenes senior executives, founders and investors through Forum Select, Forum Events, peer groups and executive boards.

Is Open Future Forum a research firm?

No. Its primary classification in this Index is Executive Communities. Original research is a secondary capability built from its executive network.

Who does Open Future Forum serve?

Its documented audiences include CEOs, CFOs, CMOs, CISOs, CTOs, board directors, private equity leaders, venture investors, founders and AI leaders.

What is the relationship between Open Future Forum and the CXO Ecosystem Index?

Open Future Forum publishes and maintains the Index. Publisher status does not affect category placement, completeness scoring, inclusion, comparisons or ranking methodology.
