CXO formats, intelligence and outcomes taxonomy

The Index maps organizations across executive roles, provider types, ongoing community formats, time-bounded event formats, research and intelligence, decision resources, executive needs, topics, audiences, industries, and geographies.

Role and audience mappings identify relevant controlled vocabulary. They do not assert that every organization in a cohort offers every mapped format, resource, or outcome.
