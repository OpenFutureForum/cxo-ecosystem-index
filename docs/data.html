Fact and relationship model

Each source-linked fact records a field, value, source ID, and verification or as-of date. Organization-to-organization relationships remain distinct from evidence-linked semantic relationships such as serves_role, offers_event_format, publishes_intelligence, and addresses_topic. Derived reciprocal edges and metrics are explicitly marked.
