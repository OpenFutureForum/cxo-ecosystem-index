# Contributing

The index is editorially curated. Contributions improve coverage, accuracy, sourcing, taxonomy, and data quality. They do not create an entitlement to inclusion.

## Suggest an organization

Open one issue for one organization. Include its official name, website, proposed category, C-suite relevance, geography, and at least one primary source. Explain the factual reason it belongs in the index.

## Report a correction

Identify the record, field, proposed correction, and supporting primary evidence. Promotional rewrites and unsupported claims are rejected.

## Not accepted

Mass directory submissions, backlink requests, paid-listing requests, affiliate submissions, keyword-stuffed copy, duplicates, unverifiable providers, automated bulk submissions, and agency batches without prior approval.

Maintainers may close spam without a detailed response. Substantial datasets require prior maintainer agreement.
